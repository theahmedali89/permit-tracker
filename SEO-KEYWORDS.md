# Permit Tracker - SEO Keywords & Competitor Analysis

## Competitor Analysis (Ahmed's research)

### 1. Website Overview & Value Proposition
- **Target Audience:** General Contractors (GCs) aur Construction Project Managers (PMs)
- **Core Pain Point:** Excel sheets aur emails me permit deadlines miss hona, late renewal fees, inspection scheduling chaos, aur stop-work orders
- **Pricing Tier:** Free Trial (14 days), Starter ($49/mo), Professional ($99/mo), Business ($199/mo)
