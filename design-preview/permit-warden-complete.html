Frequently asked questions

What is a permit tracker?

It's one dashboard for every permit, license, inspection and insurance certificate your company holds — across every city and state you work in. Instead of deadlines scattered across spreadsheets, inboxes and sticky notes, each permit shows its status, expiry date and the exact day you need to re-apply.

How do the reminders work?

Permit Warden emails you 30, 14, 7 and 1 day before each deadline, then follows up every 5 days until it's handled. Your apply-by date already subtracts the city's real processing time plus a 7-day safety buffer, and deadlines sync to Google Calendar automatically.

Which states and cities are covered?

All 50 states. The built-in AHJ directory links 186 official permit portals, and you can track deadlines from any city, county or state in one place — including processing-time data for major metros like Austin, Baltimore, Chicago and New York City.

Is there a free plan?

Yes — you can create an account and start tracking permits, licenses and inspections free, with no credit card required. Paid plans add team sharing, subcontractor COI tracking and reporting when your shop outgrows the free tier.
